About

Hi, I’m Nicole.

I’m a stand-up comedian and event host from the San Francisco Bay Area. You may have seen me on stage at the Historic Bal Theatre, at Peninsula Comedy shows, or in my short videos, usually holding a sandwich and telling the truth.

My comedy comes from real life: driving my 2003 car like it’s a race car, mixing up words in English (my second language), dreaming about winning the lottery, and finding the best noodles in town. If it happened to me, it’s going in the act.

For companies, I keep it clean, custom and kind. I joke about the things everyone in the room shares, like commutes, meetings and that printer, and nobody ends up as the punchline. I can do a headline set, host your whole program, or both.
