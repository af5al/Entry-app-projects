About Us

Welcome to Mimosa Café, a cozy little corner created for coffee lovers, food enthusiasts and beautiful moments. What started as a simple dream of bringing people together over delicious food and refreshing drinks has grown into a place where every visit feels special. From freshly brewed coffee to delightful desserts and comforting bites, we believe in serving happiness with every plate. At Mimosa, good food meets warm memories!
